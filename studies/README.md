# studies/ — design studies

Each folder is one separate study (comparison gallery or trials), with its own notes. Earlier frozen states of a study live in its `archive/` subfolder. Nothing here is loaded by the current site; a chosen direction was re-implemented in [`site/`](../site/README.md), or in [`site-b/`](../site-b/README.md) for `explorer-b-design`. The [research archive](research-archive/index.html) lists every collection except `globe-control-placement` and `explorer-b-design`.
